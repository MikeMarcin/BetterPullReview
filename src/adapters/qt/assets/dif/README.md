# Dif production source asset pack

This directory contains the canonical checked-in Dif source bytes for the
ten-clip RFC-BPR-0030 catalog.
The checked-in PNGs, not a prompt replay, are the source of truth.
`dif-sprite-manifest.json` declares how those source
frames become products. `generated/` is the committed deterministic runtime
product generated by `scripts/dif_assets.py`.

## Accepted revision

The production revision is the exact 71-frame d10 result retained under
`docs/verification/dif-production-art-v008/`. Its prop-free no-catchlight
neutral, rest, calm idle, greeting and seven extended gestures were reviewed
at their declared speed and at 64, 72 and 96 pixels on light, dark and black
surfaces. Attended-review session
`7e489c0dd362c256bc28715a16892b65` recorded twelve explicit human accept
decisions and handed the complete queue back at sequence 123 with no pending
candidate. The durable, token-free decision record is
`docs/verification/dif-production-art-v008/review/human-verdict.json`.

The same decision accepts storage and distribution of the exact 71-file d10
derivative set under the existing Dif scope without an additional attribution,
notice or third-party obligation. The received result still lacks the generator
provider, model, seed, version, exact prompt/input-role history, native layers,
pre-catchlight-removal hashes and external service terms. Those facts remain
recorded in the v008 intake instead of being guessed. The checked-in bytes and
their recorded hashes are the reproducible authority.

The earlier BPR-0287 provenance decision remains the authority for the three
supplied references and the Dif distribution scope. The d10 decision applies

Schema version 2 declares 256 x 256 RGBA8 sRGB cells, straight alpha, one
transparent gutter at each end of every strip, baseline 223, visual center 128,
16-pixel minimum edge clearance and one-pixel foot tolerance. The complete
catalog contains 71 substantive cells, 20 gutters and 23,855,104 decoded RGBA
bytes. All source images are canonical non-interlaced 8-bit RGBA PNGs with zero
RGB under zero alpha. The compiler checks inventory, symlink containment,
source and product digests, neutral handoffs, loop seams, clipping, bounds,
registration and generated descriptor compilation.

Ordinary builds consume committed products only. They do not generate art,
scan the source directory or acquire a creative dependency. Runtime selection
and Qt presentation are owned by the later application-integration milestones.

## Reproduction and review

The v008 package retains the exact external archive and extracted-file hashes,
canonical input mapping, objective measurements, preview renderer, contact
sheets, declared-speed APNGs and the protected attended candidate definitions.
Run its preparation script into an empty directory to reconstruct the accepted
source and products from the retained intake:

```sh
PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 python3 \
  docs/verification/dif-production-art-v008/prepare.py --out <empty-directory>
```

Validate and reproduce the committed tree with:

```sh
PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 python3 scripts/dif_assets.py validate \
  --assets src/adapters/qt/assets/dif
PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 python3 scripts/dif_assets.py verify \
  --assets src/adapters/qt/assets/dif
ctest --preset dev -R '^dif_assets_contract$' --output-on-failure
ctest --preset core-only -R '^dif_assets_contract$' --output-on-failure
```

Do not hand-edit generated strips or the descriptor. A changed source frame,
neutral master, manifest timing or geometry, generator, resize behavior,
generated product, preview renderer or review identity invalidates the affected
sequence-123 decision and requires a newly bound review.
