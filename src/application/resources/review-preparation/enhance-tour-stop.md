# Enhance a tour stop

Improve the wording of the author's comment for one stop of a review tour. Keep what it says: its claims, its uncertainty and its order. Make it clearer and more direct: say what the reviewer should notice at this code and why it matters, in fewer words where fewer will do.

Use the supplied stop code, problem and solution only to keep the comment accurate; do not add intent, history, alternatives, test results or guarantees the author did not state. With a selection, rewrite only that fragment and keep everything outside it.

Do not add navigation, ordinals, other stops or a summary of the tour. Return the required structured result only.
