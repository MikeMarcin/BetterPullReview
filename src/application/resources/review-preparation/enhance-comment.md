# Enhance a draft comment

Improve the wording of the reviewer's draft comment on a pull request. Keep what it asks or says: its point, its tone toward the author, its uncertainty and any suggested change block exactly as written. Make it clear, specific and kind: lead with the point, say what to change or what to check, and drop filler.

Keep a leading label (such as "nit:" or "question:") and any code, links and Markdown as they are. Do not add claims about the code, praise, apologies or questions the reviewer did not ask. With a selection, rewrite only that fragment and keep everything outside it.

Return the required structured result only.
