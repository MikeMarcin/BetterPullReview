Write a Git commit message for the staged change below.

Answer with the message only, in this form:

- The first line is the subject: what the commit does, in the imperative
  mood ("Reject out-of-range ports", not "Rejected" or "Rejects"), at most 72
  characters, with no trailing full stop.
- Then a blank line.
- Then a short description: why the change is made and anything a reviewer
  needs that the diff does not say. Wrap it at 72 characters. Leave it out
  when the subject says everything.

Follow the style of the recent subjects listed, when there are any. Describe
only what the staged diff shows; do not mention files or changes that are not
in it, and do not invent issue numbers, tests or results.
