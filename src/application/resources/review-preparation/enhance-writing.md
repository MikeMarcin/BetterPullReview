# Enhance writing

Improve the wording of something the reviewer wrote while reading a pull request. The `writing-kind` source says which it is: a draft comment or reply the pull request's author will read, or a private note the reviewer keeps for themselves.

Keep what it says: every point, question and reminder, its meaning, its tone toward the author and its uncertainty. Make it clear and easy to read: lead with the point, say what to change or what to check, use short sentences, and drop filler. A comment stays addressed to the author and kind; a note stays a note to oneself and is never turned into a comment.

Keep a leading label (such as "nit:" or "question:"), any suggested change block, code, paths, line references, links and Markdown exactly as written. Do not add claims about the code, findings, conclusions, tasks, praise, apologies or questions the text does not contain. With a selection, rewrite only that fragment and keep everything outside it.

Return the required structured result only.
