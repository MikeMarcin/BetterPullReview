# PACK-CC-26: Verify and close the selected feedback UX and icon delivery

## Outcome and work

Bind the recorded design decision and complete twelve-role source inventory to the delivered UI/resources and actual rendered interaction evidence. Preserve any explicitly required unresolved human/native verdict.

## Acceptance

- Selected—not merely candidate—art/resources and controls are the delivered ones.
- Label/state/public-private accessibility and source fidelity work in the real surfaces.
- No mockup, metadata assertion or offscreen-only check is misrepresented as native acceptance.

## Evidence and boundaries

Record the direct test/gesture, actual result, producing revision and limitations in the live card. Product evidence is **not run** at pack authoring. Follow `verification/PRODUCT-VERIFICATION.md` and current project isolation/authority rules. Focus checks on this slice; reuse unchanged evidence. Import and local fixtures grant no live GitHub, credential, model or unrelated source-change authority.
