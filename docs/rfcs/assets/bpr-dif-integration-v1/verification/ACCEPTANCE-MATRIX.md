## Ordinary product routes for the ten clips

Home exposure: greet → calm_idle; twelve seconds of eligible uninterrupted completed
breaths: blink → calm_idle; reduced/inactive: rest. Explicitly open the current AI tools
Findings section: compare_glance → rest. Start a current requested invocation through
the real launch/chat gesture: consider → rest. Deliver a validated presentable reply or
workshop proposal and accepted terminal: comment_ready. Deliver a presentable triage
batch: attention. Apply the proposal through the real accepted local-draft path:
acknowledge. Refuse/fail a current invocation, or present a real continuation choice:
blocked. Use existing deterministic process/provider fixtures, not a second mascot
launcher or a clip dropdown, to exercise these ordinary paths.

## Minimal integration evidence

Record the candidate source/resource digest once; capture the real app at 64/72/96
logical pixels as applicable, both normal themes and representative high contrast,
and 1.0/1.25/2.0 scale where supported by the test environment. This is a targeted set,
not a compulsory Cartesian product of every clip × theme × size × platform. Affected
visual edge cases drive additional captures.

Show a known frame boundary with timestamps and selected clip/frame counters; compare
a reduced-motion interval over several nominal boundaries; measure zero future mascot
wakeups after hiding/deactivation/modal/reduction and no code-region model resets or
classification. Observe one shared catalog across two windows and two profiles; restart
and verify actual persisted settings, including failed-write behavior separately.

Native Windows/macOS/Linux rows record available build/resource and observer/lifecycle
proof. Unsupported System motion observation is a valid documented capability result,
not a failed promise of support; unexecuted native tests remain unexecuted. Reuse
platform-independent art and deterministic logic evidence instead of repeating it.
