One tile per mode, six for six, in a strip that scrolls sideways inside a darker well; three and a bit are visible so the strip says it continues, and Left and Right move the selection and scroll it into view. The combo box that repeated the tiles is gone. The two System tiles split diagonally, light over dark, in their own palettes, and their caption says which half is in effect now. Sizes are shown in points, not hundredths. Each row is one line: label, an info glyph whose popover carries the consequence (the Ligatures one is open here), and the control; the card is about half its former height. Every group's Reset sits in its header. The Colours card is titled for the theme on screen and edits only that theme; the Dark tile says two of its colours are customised.

A2. Editing a colour. A role's swatch opens the picker; changes preview in the review window and a bar offers Keep or Cancel.
