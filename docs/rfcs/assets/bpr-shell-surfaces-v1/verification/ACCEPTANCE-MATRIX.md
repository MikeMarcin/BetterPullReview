and producing revision in the live card or ledger. A passed pack integrity check closes
none of these criteria. Focused automation plus the explicitly required rendered and
attended criteria suffice; do not fabricate a full suite or a native result.

## Minimal integration evidence

Capture the real application, not the mockup, at 1.0, 1.25, 1.5 and 2.0 scale in Light,
Dark, High-contrast Light and High-contrast Dark for: Accounts connected, signed out,
signing in; Home signed out, empty, listed, over a review; the sheet default, renaming,
creating, failed. That is a targeted set, not every combination of every state; affected
edge cases drive additional captures. Record the executable digest once per session.
