# Focused attended subjects

Use the current shared attended-review workflow, absolute paths for local launch and
capture commands, and one question per candidate criterion. Reuse unchanged applicable
prior evidence and preserve partial answers. These are proposed review subjects, not
verdicts. BPR-0683's accepted Settings verdict covered the window as it was; the card
page is a new subject and inherits nothing.

## Accounts & hosts (A8)

After public-path automation passes, present the real Settings window on the owner's
account, read-only: connected, then signed out, then signing in. Ask separately whether
the card says everything the old page said that mattered; whether the sign-in row is
one gesture with nothing to hunt for; and whether anything on the page repeats.

## Home (A8)

Present the real Home on first launch, then with the owner's real recent and queue rows
in the default state panels, then under Group Organization with State headers on and off,
then over an open review. Ask separately whether the bar is the obvious first thing;
whether the panels separate as the accepted boards do; whether collapsing and the
sticky headers behave; whether the account is knowable for every repository; whether
the Filter menu holds what is expected; and whether the status bar line is useful
rather than noise. The owner accepted the boards on 2026-09-13; the application is the
new subject.

## Appearance (C6)

Present the real Appearance page with the owner's account: the strip at rest and scrolled
to the high-contrast tiles, a colour edit previewing in the review window behind, and
the page after switching the desktop appearance. Ask separately whether the six tiles
read as six themes and the System split as one; whether the one-line rows with glyphs
lose anything that mattered; whether the preview shows enough to judge a change; and
whether "Colours for Dark" makes the theme binding obvious. The owner accepted the
boards on 2026-09-13; the application is the new subject.

## Profiles (B5)

Present the real sheet from Profiles › Manage Profiles…, then have the owner close the
last window and reopen from the Dock, ⌘N and the menu. Ask separately whether closing
felt like closing; whether reopening landed where expected; and whether the sheet is
the right size and shape for a thing used a few times a month.

A mockup proves only what its own layout looks like. If a subject changes after a
verdict, invalidate just that bound verdict.
