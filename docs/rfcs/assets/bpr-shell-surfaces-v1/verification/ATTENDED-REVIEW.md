# Focused attended subjects

Use the current shared attended-review workflow, absolute paths for local launch and
capture commands, and one question per candidate criterion. Reuse unchanged applicable
prior evidence and preserve partial answers. These are proposed review subjects, not
verdicts. BPR-0683's accepted Settings verdict covered the window as it was; the card
page is a new subject and inherits nothing.

## Accounts & hosts (A8)

After public-path automation passes, present the real Settings window on the owner's
account, read-only: connected, then signed out, then signing in. Ask separately whether
the card says everything the old page said that mattered; whether the sign-in row is
one gesture with nothing to hunt for; and whether anything on the page repeats.

## Home (A8)

Present the real Home signed out, empty, with the owner's real recent and queue rows,
and over an open review. Ask separately whether the bar is the obvious first thing;
whether the rows read at a glance; whether the empty sentence says where the list is
configured; and whether the status bar line is useful rather than noise.

## Profiles (B5)

Present the real sheet from Profiles › Manage Profiles…, then have the owner close the
last window and reopen from the Dock, ⌘N and the menu. Ask separately whether closing
felt like closing; whether reopening landed where expected; and whether the sheet is
the right size and shape for a thing used a few times a month.

A mockup proves only what its own layout looks like. If a subject changes after a
verdict, invalidate just that bound verdict.
