The two redrawn glyphs

Labelv3Change from v2
choreBroomCloser to the broom reference: a thinner handle, a band, a clear seam, and a broad head that fans out and is split twice from its foot. The handle, seam and splits sit on the 1× pixel diagonals.
typoSpellcheck: "Abc" over a squiggleAfter the Abc reference: a sturdy, symmetric A with straight legs, a flat apex and a flat crossbar, drawn as one filled shape; a round b and c; a smooth sine of 3.5 periods instead of the zigzag. Every stroke has the same weight.

Variants were tried for each and only the best is presented. For chore: a free-angle broom with fanned splits (clean at 2× but spotty at 1×), and lattice brooms with balanced or unequal sections. For typo: wider, bolder free-drawn letters, which ran together at 1×, and narrower lattice letters.

Legibility at 16 px

  • typo. A 12 px glyph box has room for "Abc" only if each letter keeps one clear pixel column from the next. The A is 5 px wide with its apex on the centre pixel. The b's stem, its bowl and the c are 1 px strokes on pixel edges. The A's legs (2.25 units across) carry the same weight as those strokes. The A's diagonals are the only antialiased edges, and its crossbar is a full pixel row. The squiggle reads as a wave at 1×, and is smooth at 2×.
  • chore. At 45°, edges that fall between the pixel diagonals render as whole pixel steps. So the seam between band and head, and both splits, are each one clean diagonal of lighter pixels at 1×, not a smear. The head stays one solid fan, so at 1× the broom reads as a stick with a fan.

Resource form

Unchanged from v2. It is QPainterPath data on the 24-unit grid, using M, L, C and Z plus discs. There are four element kinds: shared-pen strokes, strokes with their own pen width, filled paths and discs. The typo A is a filled path with a hole. The hole winds against the outline, so the browser's nonzero rule and QPainterPath's default odd-even rule fill it the same way. The broom's handle is now a 2.2-unit stroke. There is no SVG, font, runtime asset or build-time generation. The glyphs compile into the Qt adapter.

Provenance

The rfc-implementer agent (Claude Opus 5.5) wrote the v3 coordinates by hand for this card on 2026-10-05, in the frames' GLYPHS table. The broom is laid out on chosen pixel diagonals. Its angle and proportions follow the owner's broom reference: handle to head, band width, head spread and split depth. The letters are laid out on the pixel lattice in the style of the owner's Abc reference. The two references are committed beside this record.
