Settings and explorers, first round

Settings follows RFC-BPR-0018's seven pages and its rules: a stable sidebar, headings separated from grouped cards, a label and its consequence on every row, a reset beside the group it resets, theme previews rendered from the real palette, and nothing optimistic. Three pages are drawn in full. The explorers are the left drawer of the locked workbench: pull requests open in this window above, the active pull request's files below, each with its own filter.

4. Two drawers: repositories with their pull requests, then the file tree

Left to right: repositories, files, comparison, comments. The repositories drawer is a tree and looks like one: a repository is a bold row with a chevron and a count, its pull requests hang under it on a guide line, one per row with number, title, the unread dot and a one-word reason. The files drawer stands beside it and shows the selected pull request's tree with chevrons and guide lines, nesting folders compacted into one row. Both drawers collapse to rails; Review mode opens both, Read mode opens neither.

The line is review epochs. Each node is an update to the pull request, one push that changed what there is to review, never a commit or a file's history; the labels are the dates the review was updated. The handles compare two updates, and the amber tick is my last review. Independent collapse. Each drawer has its own toggle in the strip (the two icons at the left), its own shortcut, and its own rail with a label and a count. Collapsing one never moves the other. The modality presets set both at once; a manual toggle overrides the preset until the next preset change.

Repositories are the roots. The account is named once in the drawer header; a host shows on a repository only when it is not the header's host. A repository row counts pull requests that need you against the total. The Home link opens the full action list.
One line per pull request. Number, title, unread dot, and a one-word reason at the right (changed, requested, replied, waiting, approved, following), amber when it needs you. The full sentence is the row's tooltip and the Overview's summary. Ordered by attention within the repository.
Files beside, not below. The files drawer has its own rail and toggle. Compacted nesting folders expand a level at a time; counts on folders are files inside; the same read-state dots as the overview. Tree, flat and changed-first are the orders.
