Compose review as the lightbox, round 2

Five ways to put Finish your review over a dimmed window. Every one keeps today's one click: Comment, Approve and Request changes each submit the review, and there is no Submit review step. Review summary and the verdicts are on the right or at the bottom in all five.

The owner on round 1, verbatim. “It looks like we changed way too much compared with the existing compose review. We lost the 1 click inteface we have right now and replaced it with a 2 stage it looks like by adding Submit review. The commnets in this mockup look worse than how they look in the current app window.”

“I would like the comment box and my action to be on the right or bottom instead of the mockup's left or top.”

“I would take the existing window over this design, we should try again.”

And on this round's brief: “I mean that can be one option, but let it explore”.

  • The same in all five. It opens with the cursor in Review summary. A click on the dimmed window, Esc, Close or a mode tab closes it and keeps its words. The title bar is never dimmed: the window drags, resizes, minimises and zooms with it open (accepted in round 1). Nothing in it moves when something is clicked, and its tooltips are today's.
  • A, B, C and E change no content. Title, Esc, Expand and Close; the comments' rows; Review summary; the three verdicts; Discard review; Pull request and account; Saved comments… are drawn as the app draws them today. D changes one thing: how much of each comment shows.
