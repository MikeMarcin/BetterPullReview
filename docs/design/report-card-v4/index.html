The straight read, with a menu of sections

The report opens in its file tab with everything open and reads from the top. There is no ruler and nothing folds. One button opens a list of the report’s sections; choosing one scrolls to it, and the list marks the section being read. This round asks only where that button is and what its list is.

Where it starts: the chosen card

A · Section name: the button says where you are

A slim bar over the page. Its button is the section you are reading, and a press drops the list of headings from it, the way a pop-up button does everywhere on a Mac.

What it gives
  • You always know which section you are in without opening anything.
  • It is the standard pop-up button: nothing to learn, and it is keyboard-ready as it stands.
  • The way back to the conversation is always in view.
What it costs
  • The bar takes 32 points of the pane.
  • The list is names only: nothing says what a section holds.
  • The button’s width changes as its label does.

B · Contents: a popover that says what each section holds

The same slim bar, but the button always reads Contents, with the three lines of a menu beside it. Its list is a popover with room for more than names: each section has its first sentence under it.

What it gives
  • The list is a small digest: it says what each section holds before you go.
  • The button never changes, so it is always where you left it and the same width.
  • The conversation is one press away from anywhere in the report, in the same list as the sections.
What it costs
  • Nothing says which section you are in until you open the popover.
  • The bar takes 32 points for one button and two icons.
  • The popover is tall for a long report and needs its own scrolling. It is a new part; A’s menu is not.

C · Floating: one button, no bar

No bar at all: the page has the whole pane. One small button floats at its top right and stays there as you scroll. Everything the report can do is in its list.

What it gives
  • The most page: no bar, 32 more points of report.
  • One place for everything the report can do.
  • The button is in the same spot at any scroll position.
What it costs
  • The button sits over the page: over the top right of a wide code block or a long line as it scrolls under.
  • Three lines say nothing about what the button holds or where you are.
  • View source and Copy source are two presses instead of one.
