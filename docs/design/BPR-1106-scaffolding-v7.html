The review workbench's scaffolding, as revised

BPR-1106, design round v7, the owner's decisions of 2026-09-24 as revised the same day

The title row carries the window, the pull request and the modality, which sits at the right. A review bar reads, from its left, Updates and the comparison it names, then the active tab's controls, then Compose review and Comment at its right. Open, the timeline runs the full width above it; folded, it goes. The comparison's header is document tabs, each with its own file, comparison and position.

The window's rows at 1280×852 on macOS, half scale, measured on the frames. On Windows the same three measure 712, 712 and 746; at 1920×1080 on macOS, 935, 938 and 972.

The decisions, verbatim

From the owner's answers in the design session, after round v5:

1. For modality lets do Right.

2. Tools I like the idea of Review bar with tools and collapsable updates line, but controls should be on the left and updates line should be fully above tools when expanded.

3. T

4. I like the icons + tooltips, I think mark as read in tab tools can also be an icon with tooltip.

5. Review bar

And earlier in the same session, bringing back RFC-BPR-0025's tabs:

Let's update the design and incorporate the tabs, i think I set that aside to get something up and working but now that we have something more or less functional I want to bring them back.

Then, having seen round v6, the owner revised the review bar:

I changed my mind lets move it back to updates -> tab tools

I just meant we have the review bar which includes the tab tools and the update labels is to the left of the tab tools and the expanded review bar still has the full width update line above the update summary and tab tools

The candidates had been narrowed to "T without caption, T comment in tray, and V Tabs"; T is the first, so the Updates line loses its permanent caption. Tabs carry their own comparison, with new tabs taking the window's, as RFC-BPR-0025 specified and the owner approved on 2026-09-04.

How this was reached

Round v2 traced what the two entries do and drew four placements. Round v3 (the tray headers without tabs) was superseded in the session by round v4, which added the tabs and the vertical rail. Round v5 tried the modality's places, icons, three homes for the tab's controls and the fold. Round v6 drew the answers; this page draws them as the owner revised them.

The frames are drawn from the accepted surface's tokens and the product's measured rows, rendered by headless Chrome at exact window size, device scale 1, reduced to 256 colours and looked at; every height is read back from the page's layout. The Windows frames use the Mac's type.
