The split

InlineThis file's threads as cards beside their lines. A card shows its first post in full, with its mark and rail (BPR-1083 v4), the people, the reply count and what is new. It opens in place to the whole conversation and a Reply field. Threads above or below the visible code sit as chips on the top and bottom edges; clicking one scrolls the code there. When no thread is on the visible lines, the nearest one each way peeks in. The code's scroll bar keeps a tick per thread.
ConversationOne conversation, read in full: every post, its reply structure, a New rule, and Reply at the foot. The header picks the thread (a list of the whole pull request, by file), steps to the previous or next one, shows its line, resolves it, marks it read. It follows the thread chosen in Inline.
Notes, TourUnchanged.
GoneThe "Conversations drawer" caption, the pill graph, the "Path to this reply" line, the seven-line post list and the separate body pane.

Conversation

Choice 1: how a conversation shows who answers whom

GitHub review threads look the same both ways: every reply answers the first comment, so replies sit one step in. The two differ only when a conversation branches, as branching-thread-v1 does (24 posts; three branches off the first comment; branch C forks at its third reply).

A. IndentedA reply sits under the post it answers. A run where each post answers the one above stays level. A line joins a post with several replies to each of them, and a ⊖ on that line folds the branch.

B. ↩ chipEvery post in order, one step in. A reply that answers anything but the post right above it starts with "↩ name: first words"; hovering highlights that post and clicking goes to it.

Choice 2: clicking a card in Inline

A. Opens in placeThe card grows into the whole conversation with Reply, as in the first Inline frame. Its ↗ icon opens it in Conversation.

B. Opens ConversationCards stay closed; a click switches to the Conversation tab on that thread, with its line still shown in the code. The card's ⌄ still opens it in place.
