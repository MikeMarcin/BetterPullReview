White space, the Sublime Text way

BPR-1107, design v1 — for the owner's review. Every picture below is the real product drawing, captured offscreen from the review panes and the Appearance page as built; nothing is mocked.

What the owner asked for (attended session cbd48b91, decision 6b7243c3)

Should follow the sublime text model with dots and lines and have settings to control white space. We don't need to support all their settings but at least easy and impactful ones.

The full note quotes Sublime Text's draw_white_space documentation; it is on the card.

1. The drawing, beside the owner's reference

A space is a small dot centred in its cell. A tab is a thin line from where it starts to its tab stop, so a tab that begins partway to a stop is shorter ("Another tab, smaller because of spacing"). Both are drawn in the code_line_number_text role at a faint alpha, the colour the old tab markers used; no new role. The review panes, the Appearance preview and the theme tiles all draw through one shared painter.

The four themes (All)

The offscreen test host resolves the code font to a proportional face, so letter widths differ from a real monospaced setup; the dots and lines are placed wherever the editor puts each character, so they follow whatever font is in effect.

2. The setting: when white space is drawn

One row on the Type card, directly beneath Tab width, in RFC-BPR-0034's row language: label, info glyph, control. It is typography, kept per profile in the appearance store (schema version 8, with a migration).

The six choices

ChoiceSublime TextDraws
None[]Nothing.
Under the selection proposed default["selection"] (Sublime's default)Every space and tab on the lines the selection washes, nothing elsewhere.
Leading["leading"]Indentation: from the start of a line to its first other character.
Trailing["trailing"]From a line's last other character to its end: the stray white space a review most often wants to see.
Leading and trailing["leading", "trailing"]Both, and so a line of white space alone (Sublime's "isolated").
All["all"]Everything, including white space enclosed between words.

The reader is on line 1, so Under the selection draws on line 1 of both sides.

3. The proposed default: Under the selection

It is Sublime Text's own default, which the owner pointed to. It keeps a review calm: nothing is drawn until the reader selects, and then every tab and space of what they selected shows, which is where the question "is that a tab or spaces?" is asked. A profile saved before this change takes the same default when its store is migrated. The alternative worth weighing is Trailing, which would flag stray end-of-line white space in every diff without a gesture. The owner confirms or changes the default.

4. What is left out, and why

  • Combining choices (Sublime's list, e.g. ["all_tabs", "selection"]): one choice at a time keeps the row one line and legible. "Leading and trailing" is the one combination that earns its own entry.
  • Enclosed and isolated on their own: enclosed alone answers no review question All does not; isolated is already drawn by Leading, Trailing and Leading and trailing, because a line of white space alone is both.
  • The _tabs, _spaces and _none refinements: each would multiply the six choices by three for a small gain; add them later if the owner asks.
  • The _mixed refinements: they need an indentation style ("translate tabs to spaces"), which a review window reading someone else's code does not have.
  • Indentation guides (the faint vertical lines in the reference): a separate Sublime setting, not white space; not built here.
