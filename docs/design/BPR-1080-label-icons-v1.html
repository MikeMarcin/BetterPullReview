Label icons, candidates v1

BPR-1080 (RFC-BPR-0050; R30, R31). 2026-10-05. Frames: BPR-1080-label-icons-frames-v1.html. Marks as selected in BPR-1079.

Candidates only. Nothing is approved or implemented. Each icon replaces its label's two letters inside the accepted 16 px mark: a soft tinted square normally, filled with the tint when blocking, under the avatar with the label rail. Tints are unchanged.

Every label has its own glyph, with no aliases. Related labels have unrelated shapes: suggestion (bulb) and polish (sparkles), and nitpick (magnifier) and quibble (arrows). Alternatives are offered only where a second metaphor works as well at 16 px. Tweezers for nitpick and scales for quibble were tried and dropped, because at 1× they read as a pen and as a blot.

Identification without colour or hover

  • The glyph's shape carries the label, and the tint only adds to it. The greyscale rows in the sheet keep all twelve apart.
  • Blocking is shown by the fill and the 4 px rail, not by colour.
  • Each mark is an image named with its label and decoration, for example "issue (blocking)", and its tooltip shows the same words, as BPR-1079 accepted.
  • The Label key (⌥K) is reachable from the keyboard and lists every mark with its name and shortcode. The ⌘L menu and completion show the name beside the mark.
  • The label's public text stays the source. Copy source and GitHub get the words, never the icon.

Resource form

The glyphs are path data on the app's existing stroked-glyph grid: 24 units, a round-capped pen, as workbench_strip.cpp and review_cycle_share_marks.cpp already draw. The data uses only M, L, C and Z plus filled discs, so it maps one-to-one onto QPainterPath moveTo, lineTo, cubicTo, closeSubpath and addEllipse. The Qt adapter paints each glyph into the mark at 1×, 1.25× and 2× in the label's ink. The pen is 2.6 units: 1.3 px in the 12 px glyph box at 1×.

The glyphs need no SVG files, Qt SVG module, icon font, bundled font, runtime download or build-time generation, and add no separate package asset. The glyphs compile into the Qt adapter. BPR-1081 binds the selected path data to its source revision and its place in that adapter.

Provenance

Measured

?check=1 on the frozen source, from headless Chrome:

  • Separation. Each glyph was rasterised alone with the shipping pen, and every pair from different labels was compared by ink overlap (1 − intersection / union). The closest pair at 12 px is star and swap, at 0.54. At 24 px it is issue and note, at 0.48: the two share a stem, with the dot at opposite ends. Suggestion and polish measure 0.79. Nitpick and quibble measure 0.69 with swap and 0.86 with plus-minus.
  • Contrast. The glyph against its soft fill is at least 6.06:1 (typo, dark). Against a blocking fill it is at least 4.52:1 (polish, light). Both minimums cover all twelve tints in the four theme variants.
  • Placement. The thread frames keep v4's placement: the mark is centred under the avatar (0 px offset, 3 px gap), the rail sits 3 px away, blocking posts have the fill and the 4 px rail, the names match, and no header holds a mark.
