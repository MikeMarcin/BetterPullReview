Rules every surface keeps

  • One conversation counts once, classified by its root. Replies carry their own marks but never add to a count.
  • Other is the remainder: chips plus Other equal the included conversations. A last row, Not on a file, holds unanchored conversations.
  • Labels are square and states are round. Colour adds to a mark and never carries meaning alone.
  • A label is public text at the start of the comment. The preview shows exactly what GitHub gets, and Copy source keeps the prefix. A shortcode is never sent. Private defects keep their dashed, locked badge.
  • No label resolves, approves or requests changes. A suggested-change block keeps its own header and apply affordance.
