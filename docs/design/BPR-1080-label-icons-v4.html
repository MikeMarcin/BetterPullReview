Label icons, candidates v4

BPR-1080 (RFC-BPR-0050; R30, R31). 2026-10-05. Frames: BPR-1080-label-icons-frames-v4.html. Earlier rounds: v1, v2, v3. Marks as selected in BPR-1079.

Candidates only. Nothing is implemented, and typo is not approved. Each icon replaces its label's two letters inside the accepted 16 px mark. Tints are unchanged.

The redrawn glyph

Labelv4Change from v3
typoSpellcheck: "Abc" over a squiggleSet as one word. The A keeps its bold, symmetric shape and its flat apex on the centre pixel, but stands half a pixel narrower on each side, with heavier legs. The b's stem now follows the A at once, so the gap closes towards the baseline as it does in the Abc reference. The c is a whole, open c, three pixels wide, and stops a pixel short of the mark's border. All three letters are filled shapes. The crossbar sits a pixel lower, which opens a counter in the A. The sine squiggle is v3's.

Variants were tried internally and only the best is presented. They included v3's A with the b kerned onto its foot, which merged into a dark block at 1×; a narrower A, which lost its weight; a word spread over the full 24 units, which put the b on half pixels and blurred it at 1×; a c reaching the box edge, which looked clipped again; and heavier b and c strokes, which greyed the b's counter.

Legibility at 16 px

  • Why v3's c looked cut off. The 1× glyph box is 12 px. Five pixels of A, three of b and three of c with a clear pixel between each need 13. v3 kept both gaps and the full A, so the c got two pixels and ran into the border.
  • Where v4 finds the pixel. The A's feet sit at half pixels, so its outer columns are softened rather than dropped, and the b's stem takes the next pixel column. The A's leg narrows away from the b as it rises, which keeps the two apart where it matters, and the pair reads as one word. The b keeps a one-pixel counter, a clear column parts b from c, and the c has three columns with a pixel of panel before the border.
  • Checked at 1×, pixel by pixel. The A reads as apex, shoulders, an open counter, the bar on its own row, then feet. The b reads as stem, bowl and counter. The c's terminals are three-quarter-strength pixels, an open end rather than a cut one. All four theme variants were checked, normal and blocking. At 2× and 2.5× the letters are crisp, and the A has a visible counter.

Resource form

Unchanged in kind. It is QPainterPath data on the 24-unit grid, using M, L, C and Z plus discs, as shared-pen strokes, strokes with their own pen width, filled paths and discs. The typo letters are now three filled paths. The A's and b's holes wind against their outlines, so the browser's nonzero rule and QPainterPath's default odd-even rule fill them the same way. The squiggle stays a 2-unit stroke. There is no SVG, font, runtime asset or build-time generation. The glyphs compile into the Qt adapter.
