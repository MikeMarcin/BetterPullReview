Legibility at 16 px

  • typo. Three letters have to fit in a 12 px glyph box, so v2 draws them with a 2-unit pen, which is exactly 1 px at 1×, and puts every stroke centre on an odd unit, which is a pixel centre at 1×. Each letter then has crisp stems, an open counter and one clear pixel column before the next. The squiggle is a 45° zigzag through pixel centres, three rows high. A free-drawn "Abc" at 2.6 units ran together into "Albc" at 1×, and that is why the pen and lattice differ for this one glyph.
  • nitpick. The ring is a 2.6-unit stroke, and the handle is a 4.5-unit filled bar. The heavier handle is what reads as a loupe at 1×.
  • chore. The handle is a stroke, and the band and head are filled. The two slits are 1.7 units wide, so about 1 px at 1× and 2 px at 2×. At 1× they show as lighter seams, and the head still reads as a broom.
  • quibble. These are strokes with the shared pen. The rays stop 6.4 units short of the hump, so they never merge with it.

Identification without colour or hover

  • The glyph's shape carries the label, and the tint only adds to it. The greyscale rows in the sheet keep all twelve apart.
  • Blocking is shown by the fill and the 4 px rail, not by colour.
  • Each mark is an image named with its label and decoration, for example "issue (blocking)", and its tooltip shows the same words, as BPR-1079 accepted.
  • The Label key (⌥K) is reachable from the keyboard and lists every mark with its name and shortcode. The ⌘L menu and completion show the name beside the mark.
  • The label's public text stays the source. Copy source and GitHub get the words, never the icon.

Resource form

The glyphs are path data on the app's existing stroked-glyph grid: 24 units, a round-capped pen, as workbench_strip.cpp and review_cycle_share_marks.cpp already draw. The data uses only M, L, C and Z plus filled discs, so it maps one-to-one onto QPainterPath moveTo, lineTo, cubicTo, closeSubpath and addEllipse. A glyph is a list of elements of four kinds. A plain path is stroked with the shared pen. A path with its own width is stroked with that pen (typo, 2.0). A filled path is drawn with QPainter::fillPath and no pen (the loupe's handle, and the broom's band and head). A disc is drawn with addEllipse. The Qt adapter paints each glyph into the mark at 1×, 1.25× and 2× in the label's ink. The pen is 2.6 units: 1.3 px in the 12 px glyph box at 1×.

The glyphs need no SVG files, Qt SVG module, icon font, bundled font, runtime download or build-time generation, and add no separate package asset. The glyphs compile into the Qt adapter. BPR-1081 binds the selected path data to its source revision and its place in that adapter.

Provenance

The rfc-implementer agent (Claude Opus 5.5) wrote every coordinate by hand for this card on 2026-10-05, in the frames' GLYPHS table. In round v2 the broom and loupe points were laid out from hand-chosen axes, widths and lengths, and the typo letters on the pixel lattice. The owner's two images (broom, small wobble) are committed beside this record as owner references. They set the style the owner asked for; nothing was traced or sampled from them. No icon set, font, tracing, image generation or third-party artwork was used. The metaphors are common conventions, such as a heart, a question mark or a light bulb, and no particular artwork is copied. The data belongs to this repository under its licence. Nothing comes from a chat sketch (R30).

Measured

?check=1 on the frozen source, from headless Chrome, over all eight frames, light and dark (16 dumps):

  • Separation. Each of the twelve glyphs was rasterised alone with its own pens, and every pair was compared by ink overlap (1 − intersection / union). The closest pair is still issue and note, unchanged from v1: 0.57 at 12 px and 0.48 at 24 px. The four redrawn glyphs are at least 0.66 from their nearest neighbour at 12 px (chore to note) and 0.69 at 24 px. Typo and quibble, which both have a wavy line, measure 0.81 and 0.79. Nitpick and quibble measure 0.77 and 0.73.
  • Contrast. The tints and ink are unchanged, so the minimums are the same as v1. The glyph against its soft fill is at least 6.06:1 (typo, dark), and against a blocking fill at least 4.52:1 (polish, light). These cover all twelve labels in the four theme variants. The sheet shows every glyph normal and blocking in light, dark, high-contrast light and high-contrast dark.
  • Placement. v4's placement is unchanged: the mark is centred under the avatar (0 px offset, 3 px gap), the rail sits 3 px away, blocking posts have the fill and the 4 px rail, the names match, and no header holds a mark.
