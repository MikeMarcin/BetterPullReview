A · Roster

Agents as a short grouped list, the way System Settings lists accounts. Each row says who the agent is, what it runs with and whether it works. Clicking a row opens its sheet; nothing in the list is a button.

What it optimizes
Calm and height. Three agents take 150 points, so Built-in prompts start above the middle of the window. A healthy agent is one quiet line; the broken one says the fix where its settings were.
What it gives up
Model, effort, scratch folder and builds are one click away, in the sheet. Comparing two agents' settings means reading two summary lines.
