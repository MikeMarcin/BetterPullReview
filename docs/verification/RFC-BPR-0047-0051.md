### What exists now

`bpr/core/feedback_convention.hpp` is the one catalog and recognizer, in
`better_pull_review_core`, with no Qt, provider, filesystem, clock or other effect.

- **Catalog.** `feedback_label_catalog()` holds the twelve IDs in the pack's order, each
  with the same fields: ID, description, guidance and icon role
  (`feedback.label.<id>`). `find_feedback_label` matches an exact ID, ASCII
  case-insensitively, and nothing else; `nit`, `typos` and `polishing` are not labels.
- **Result.** `recognize_feedback_prefix(body)` returns the five pack statuses and keeps
  label, catalog guidance and declared expectation as separate fields. The annotation
  also carries raw decorations, each typed `custom`, `blocking`, `non_blocking` or
  `if_minor`, plus diagnostics. Spans are UTF-8 byte offsets into the exact body:
  user body, raw label, header, subject and discussion. The header ends where the
  subject begins. The result also carries the body's SHA-256, parser revision 1 and
  the bytes inspected. It never rewrites the body.
- **Managed envelopes.** `recognize_feedback_segment(body, span)` reads only a span the
  owner validated: `map_tour_stop(...).Narrative`, or the permalink prose at
  `body.size() - Prose.size()`. With no span, or one outside the body, it returns
  `plain` with `classification_unavailable`.

### Decisions recorded here (grammar revision 1)

| Decision | Choice |
|---|---|
| Classifier budget | The header, from the user body's start through the subject's first byte, must lie in the first **1024 bytes**. Past it the body is `limited` with `classifier_budget_exceeded`: no label, expectation `unspecified`, body untouched. The subject line itself is not bounded, so a long one-line comment is still recognized. |
| Decoration count | At most **32**; the 33rd makes the body `limited`. |
| Label word | 1–32 bytes of `[A-Za-z][A-Za-z0-9_-]*`. A longer leading word is prose (`plain`). |
| Separators | A colon directly after the label or `)`. Then one or more spaces or tabs and a nonblank subject on the same line. Spaces or tabs are allowed before `(` and around commas. `label : x`, `label:x` and `label (a) : x` are malformed. |
| Known versus unknown word | Broken syntax after a catalog label is `malformed`, with one diagnostic and the raw token kept. The same breakage after any other word is ordinary prose (`plain`), so `https://…`, `Summary:` followed by a list, and `Hmm (see above):` classify nothing. A well-formed header with a word outside the catalog is `unknown_label`: decorations are kept, expectation stays `unspecified`. |
| Standard decorations | Exactly `blocking`, `non-blocking` and `if-minor`, ASCII case-insensitive. `non_blocking` and `nonblocking` are custom text. |
| Guidance contradictions (advisory `label_expectation_conflict`) | `praise`, `nitpick`, `thought` and `quibble` declared `blocking`. `note` declared `blocking` or `if-minor`. `todo`, `typo` and `chore` declared `non-blocking`. No contradiction changes status or expectation. |
| Duplicates | Any repeated token, compared ASCII case-insensitively, adds `duplicate_decoration`. Identical standard tokens do not conflict. |

### What exists now

All of it is in `better_pull_review_core`, with no Qt, provider, filesystem, clock
or other effect.

- **Body segment.** `feedback_body_segment` says which bytes are the user's: the
  whole body, or a managed envelope's validated span, or none.
  `recognize_feedback_body` dispatches on it. Edits and the derived set use it, so
  a tour stop or permalink is handled by the same rule everywhere.
- **Header edits** (`feedback_header_edit.hpp`). `decide_feedback_header_edit`
  takes the current body, its segment, the SHA-256 and parser revision of the
  body the author was shown, and one edit. An edit keeps, sets or removes the
  label, and may set the standard expectation. The decision names one replaced
  UTF-8 byte span of the old body, the replacement, the new body, the shifted
  segment and the new body's annotation. The decision is pure. A caller applies
  `Body` through the existing revisioned draft `edit` command, or an editor
  converts `Replaced` to its own indexes. That binding is BPR-1082 and the Qt
  conversion is BPR-1083.
- **Roots and the derived set** (`feedback_conversation.hpp`).
  `classify_feedback_conversation` finds the root from reply linkage alone.
  `refresh_feedback_annotations` recomputes one scope's derived set from a
  complete accepted observation and returns a delta. `adopt_feedback_refresh`
  applies it only onto the exact set it was computed against. The scope key is
  the existing `cached_review_key`: authority, profile, host, account,
  repository and pull request.

### Decisions recorded here

| Decision | Choice |
|---|---|
| Revision binding | An edit carries the body digest and parser revision it was chosen against. Any difference is `stale_revision`: nothing is applied, and the decision reports the current body's annotation so the editor can plan again. The draft's own `ExpectedRevision` and scope still guard the write. |
| Edited span | Relabel or decoration change: the whole header, from the label (or its `**`) to the first subject byte. Removal: the same span, replaced by nothing. Plain body: an empty span at the first byte of the first nonblank line, after a BOM, blank lines and at most three spaces. |
| Canonical form | Only the edited header is rewritten. A new label is written lowercase, decorations are joined with `, `, and one space follows the colon. The author's form (plain, `**label:**`, `**label**:`) is kept. A label that is not being changed keeps its spelling. |
| No-op | A request that changes nothing is `none` and not `Changed`. Examples: keeping everything, setting the label already there, or setting the expectation the tokens already declare exactly. The body is not rewritten into canonical form. |
| Decorations | Custom tokens stay in order. The first standard token becomes the target and later standard tokens are dropped. With none, the target is appended. `unspecified` removes standard tokens and drops `()` when the list empties. `conflicting` is not a target. |
| Plain body | Adding a label writes the header before the first line. It refuses with `restructuring_required` when that line opens a quote, list, ATX heading, fence, HTML, table, thematic break or link reference definition, is indented code, or is empty. Nothing is deleted to make room. |
| Read-back | Every result is recognized again. It must give the requested label (or the kept custom one), the exact decoration tokens, the requested expectation and a header exactly where it was written. Otherwise the edit is refused with `restructuring_required`. This covers a header pushed past the 1024-byte budget. A removal that would expose a second catalog label, such as `issue: note: x`, is refused the same way. |
| Refused headers | `malformed`, `limited` and an envelope with no validated segment are `unsupported_header`; the author fixes them by hand. |
| Size | A result over the shared draft's 64 KiB bound (`maximum_shared_draft_body_bytes`) is `body_limit`. |
| Root | Exactly one post in the conversation has no reply target, and every reply's chain reaches it without a cycle or an unobserved link. Input order, identifier order, recency and reply labels play no part. |
| Root status | `labelled`, `unknown_label` (custom label) and `plain` are known; `unknown_label` and `plain` are the catch-all. `unclassified` (malformed, limited, envelope without segment) is partial. `root_missing`, `root_deleted` and `root_ambiguous` are unknown, and no reply is promoted. |
| Reuse key | Scope, post identity, body SHA-256, segment and parser revision. A deleted post has no annotation. After a scope replacement nothing is reused. |
| Delta | `Recognized` lists the posts parsed again. `Removed` lists posts no longer observed or now deleted. `ChangedConversations` lists only conversations whose root, status or label changed, appeared or vanished. |
| Staleness | Each adopted refresh advances `Epoch`. A refresh whose base epoch or scope differs from the current set is refused, and the set is kept. |
| Duplicates | A post identity observed twice, or in two conversations, refuses the whole refresh (`duplicate_post`). Deduplicating repeated pages belongs to BPR-1078 and BPR-1087. |

  Unicode subject, and the CRLF parse-only no-op. Every byte after the header is
  checked identical.
- **Round trips.** Every catalog label is relabelled to every catalog label and back,
  in the three forms, with three decoration lists and three tails (CRLF with
  Unicode and a patch, LF with an empty fence, no newline): 3,888 cases, each
  restoring the original bytes. Removing and re-adding each label adds 36 more.
- **Explicit edits.** Custom decorations are kept in place, standard tokens are
  replaced, added and removed, and a conflict is settled only by an explicit edit.
  Strong forms and spelling are kept. A leading BOM, blank lines and indent survive
  removal. Nine no-op requests, canonical or not, change nothing.
- **Plain insertion.** Eight bodies take a header: `#123`, bold, inline code,
  `1.5`, `-1`, a link, `Summary:` before a list, and a setext title. Twenty
  block starts and empty bodies are refused. A header pushed past the classifier
  budget is refused. The 64 KiB bound is tested at the limit and one byte past
  it.
- **Revision binding (R41).** Another digest or parser revision is
  `stale_revision`. Composed with the real `revisioned_review_draft`: the author
  types after the edit is planned, and the old command is refused with `conflict`
  while the newer draft is kept byte for byte. The old plan is also refused
  against the new body, and replanning applies cleanly. A command for another
  draft scope is refused with `invalid_identity` and the draft is untouched.
- **Managed envelope.** A CRLF tour stop from `render_tour_stop` is labelled inside
  its `map_tour_stop` narrative. The owner maps the edited stop again to exactly the
  shifted segment, and removal restores the published stop byte for byte. The whole
  envelope as ordinary text, and an envelope with no segment, are refused.
- **Generated.** 30,000 bodies (splitmix64, seed 1077) with random edits. The
  invariants hold for each: one replaced span inside the user body on code point
  boundaries, the old body outside it unchanged, the result within the bound, the
  annotation of the new body, and determinism. Nothing before the header and
  nothing from the subject on changes. Every applied edit is idempotent.

### Decisions recorded here

| Decision | Choice |
|---|---|
| Unit | One conversation identity, the same one the annotation set uses. Its bucket is its root classification only. |
| Repeated observations | Identical facts collapse, for posts (BPR-1077's open item) and for conversations. Any differing fact refuses the whole refresh or population (`duplicate_post`, `contradictory_duplicate`), because no observation says which copy is current. |
| Inclusion | Only `published` and not deleted. A deleted root in a surviving thread stays in, as unclassified catch-all, with its own resolution. |
| Resolution | Four disjoint states, `unknown` when the provider has none (an anonymous REST group); none is a default for another. The default mode is `unresolved` (`default_feedback_resolution_mode`); `all` includes every state. `States` covers the whole scope in both modes, so excluded units stay visible. The exact name of the `all` mode in the UI is RFC-BPR-0050's. |
| Catch-all | Unselected labels, plain, custom and unclassified roots, each counted separately in `CatchallParts`. Partial classification is never folded into plain. |
| Attribution | `file` (exact path, outdated still counts), `unanchored` (top-level, review summary, path-less thread), `unknown` (lost anchor, uncertain rename). Only `file` enters a file or folder; folders match whole path components. The pull request holds every unit, and `Unattributed` is the remainder file rows cannot show. |
| Limits | `collection_partial`, `collection_stale`, `collection_unavailable`; `classification_partial` when any root in the scope is unclassified (any state, matching the pack oracle); `resolution_unknown` in unresolved mode when any unit's state is unknown; `attribution_unknown` for a file or folder when the population holds any unknown anchor, because that unit could belong to any file. A complete empty scope is an exact zero. |
| Refusals | Empty identity, an anchor whose path does not match its status or is not a clean relative path, an invalid set scope, an invalid count scope, a column selected twice. |
| Conservation | `Included` is summed over all rows independently of the column split, so dedicated plus catch-all equalling it is checked, not assumed. |

### What exists now

All of it is in `better_pull_review_core` (`feedback_draft_edit.hpp`), with no
effect. Nothing in `src/application`, `src/adapters` or `src/app` changed.

- **Comment drafts.** `plan_feedback_draft_edit` takes the current
  `revisioned_review_draft`, the basis the author was shown (revision, body
  digest, parser revision) and one header edit. It returns the decision and, when
  the body changes, the command the draft owner already accepts: an `edit` with
  the whole new body, the draft's scope, the shown revision and no composition
  link. That command takes the path a typed edit takes:
  `review_composition_application_host::change_draft` and
  `shared_draft_application_host::adopt` for a durable draft, `apply` for a
  volatile one.
- **Tour stops.** `plan_feedback_tour_stop_edit` returns the existing
  `edit_stop` command with only the stop's body changed. `review_tour_host`
  executes it for the desktop and for `bpr tour` alike.
- **Label first.** `decide_feedback_composition_edit` is the header decision
  for a body being written.

### Decisions recorded here

| Decision | Choice |
|---|---|
| Authority | The Markdown body is the only label authority. No draft record, store column or window state holds a label, decoration or annotation. A restart recognizes the saved bytes again. |
| Basis | A plan needs the shown revision and the shown body digest to equal the current ones. Either difference is `stale_draft`; nothing is applied, and the decision carries the current body's annotation, which is the conflict candidate. A reply-target change alone still makes the plan stale. |
| Prepared action | Only a `drafting` draft is planned. `publishing`, `published` and `discarded` are `not_editable`. The prepared proposal, its grant and its idempotency key keep the exact body, including after a restart and a retry after a lost answer. |
| Linked drafts | The command carries no composition link. The durable owner records the reviewer successor; the volatile reducer refuses linked prose, as before. |
| Label first | This revises a derived rule of BPR-1077, which refused an empty body. In an ordinary body that is blank (a BOM, spaces, tabs, line breaks), or exactly a header with an empty subject and nothing after it, the edit is decided against the body plus one probe subject byte, and that byte is removed afterwards. Read-back is the BPR-1077 check on the probe. Managed envelopes and every other body go to `decide_feedback_header_edit` unchanged. |
| Tour stop | The stop's raw body field is an ordinary body. A read-only tour is `not_editable`, and an unknown stop is `stale_draft`. |
| No-op | A request that changes nothing plans no command, so the revision does not move. |

recognizer's `Expectation`, and gave each standard decoration its own look.
