### BPR-1144: cycle identities and transitions

Owned paths: `src/core/include/bpr/core/review_cycle.hpp`,
`src/core/src/review_cycle.cpp`, `tests/core/review_cycle_tests.cpp`, their
two CMake registrations, and the
[domain model](../architecture/domain-and-state-model.md#review-cycle-identities-rfc-bpr-0053-in-progress)
section. No existing type changed; `pull_request_authority` is held whole as
one alternative of `review_subject`.

`ctest --test-dir build/dev -R "^core_review_cycle$"` passes (macOS arm64,
Homebrew LLVM dev preset), and the same test passes under the `core-only`
preset with Apple clang. It covers: a cycle created with no pull request,
remote, commit, account or agent; uncaptured versus an empty complete R0;
zero profile/incarnation, foreign profile, foreign incarnation, stale
generation and foreign-cycle references refused; cycle and revision counter
exhaustion; stale expected revisions; archive/unarchive; identical recapture
as a no-op versus a deliberate handoff observation with the same bytes;
partial capture, narrowed scope and unavailable paths classified unknown
rather than deleted; canonical scope paths; frozen rounds reused only for the
same draft revision and digest; recipient claims kept apart from reviewer
decisions. `architecture_boundaries` and `architecture_boundary_contract`
pass with the new core files, and clang-tidy passes on them.

Limits: RC53-01's "reopen" and RC53-02's "persist" need the store; BPR-1145
supplies that evidence. RC53-03's application ports are BPR-1146's.

The `core-only` preset does not build at this base for an unrelated reason:
`tests/core/agent_profile_tests.cpp:138` asserts a layout size that Apple

### BPR-1147: local scope and disclosure policy primitives

Owned paths: `src/core/include/bpr/core/review_cycle_policy.hpp`,
`src/core/src/review_cycle_policy.cpp`, `tests/core/review_cycle_policy_tests.cpp`,
their CMake registrations, and the Review Cycles section of the
[threat model](../architecture/security-threat-model.md#review-cycles-registration-proposed).

`ctest --test-dir build/dev -R "^core_review_cycle_policy$"` passes. Its
31-row policy table covers an unknown-origin folder (local capture and OS
destinations allowed, external retrieval and adapters unconfigured), known
enterprise data (one configured allowance opens only that channel; a denial
closes it and does not leak to other classifications), denied file, terminal
and external-read channels, the response-only grant (read and append allowed;
verify, export, publish, capture and implementation instruction reviewer-only),
imported content refused for every capability even with a grant attached, and
publication refused until its plan is confirmed. Further checks bind a grant
to one subject, round and digest, honor revocation, refuse a consumer-chosen
export channel, keep classification from downgrading on reopen, and read
"verified", "approved" and command-bearing report text as no claim.
`architecture_boundaries`, `architecture_boundary_contract` and tidy pass.

Limits: these are core decisions. Wiring them into the owner channel, the CLI,
export and adapters belongs to the later milestones that add those surfaces;
RC60-04 and RC60-05 stay open for those surfaces until then.
